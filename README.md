# 🔮 Kiwi Quest

**Turn your coursework into an adventure.**

Input your course outlines and assignments, and AI organizes them into quests with deadlines. The most urgent tasks are revealed through *The Kiwizard*'s crystal ball, so you always know exactly what to tackle first. Work smarter, not harder. 😉
